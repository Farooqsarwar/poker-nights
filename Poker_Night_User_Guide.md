# Poker Night — Complete User Guide

Welcome to the Poker Night application! This guide explains exactly how to use the app depending on your role. The application is divided into three distinct experiences:
1. **Admin (Organizer)**
2. **Registered Group Member (Player)**
3. **New Guest (+1, +2, etc.)**

---

## 1. The Admin Experience
*The Admin is the person who creates the group, organizes the event, and operates the live tournament.*

### Creating an Event
1. From the **Home** tab, tap **New Tournament** (or the quick action in the sidebar).
2. Follow the guided wizard to configure your event:
   * **Game Info**: Set the name, date, time, buy-in, and target duration.
   * **Rules**: Configure rebuys (when they close, how much they cost) and add-ons. You can also toggle KO Bounties.
   * **Chip Set**: Tell the app what physical chips you own (colors, values, and exact quantities). The engine will use this to generate the perfect blind structure!
3. Review the preview card, and tap **Publish Event**. This opens RSVPs and notifies your group.

### Managing RSVPs & Check-In
1. Once published, your main dashboard button becomes **Review RSVPs**. Here you can see who is Confirmed, who is bringing Guests (Going +2), and who is a Maybe.
2. On the day of the event, tap **Open Check-in**.
3. As players arrive, they will tap "Check In" on their devices. You will receive these requests in your **Check-in Queue**. Tap to confirm them, which will lock in their attendance.

### Generating the Structure & Starting
1. Once everyone is checked in, tap **Generate Final Structure**.
2. Poker Night will calculate the mathematically perfect starting stack, blind progression, level duration (10, 15, or 20 mins), and expected finish time based on your target duration and exact chip inventory.
3. Review the seating assignments and the generated structure, then tap **Start Tournament**.

### Operating the Live Tournament
During the game, your screen is the command center:
* **Timer Controls**: Pause, Resume, or jump to the Next Level.
* **Player Actions**: Tap the **Players** tab to record Eliminations, Rebuys, or Add-ons. 
* **Rebuy Break**: When the rebuy period ends, the timer automatically pauses. The app will guide you through the settlement workflow, calculating add-ons and finalizing the Prize Pool.
* **Final Table & Results**: When down to 9 players, the app will generate a final table redraw. When the tournament finishes, the app calculates all payout positions privately.

## 2. The Registered Group Member Experience
*A signed-in user who belongs to the permanent group. You have access to group chat, polls, and full event tracking.*

### RSVP and Preparation
1. When an Admin publishes an event, you will receive a notification and see the event pinned in the **Group Chat**.
2. Navigate to the event and choose your RSVP: **Going**, **Maybe**, **Can't Come**, or **Going +N** (if you are bringing guests).
3. If you choose **Going +1** (or +2, +3, +4), the app will create "Unclaimed Guest Slots" for you. You can share the event link or QR code with your friends so they can claim those slots.

### Checking In
1. When you arrive at the game, open the app. If the Admin has opened check-in, your main button will boldly say **Check In**.
2. Tap it, and wait for the Admin to confirm you.
3. Once confirmed, the app will reveal your **Table and Seat assignment**. 

### The Live Game
1. During the game, your main button becomes **Open Live Tournament**.
2. Your screen is a customized, read-only dashboard. You can track:
   * The live timer, current blinds, and next blinds.
   * Players remaining and the average chip stack.
   * The growing Prize Pool.
3. **Note**: For security, you cannot see admin controls, total rebuy/add-on money pools, or individual payout amounts. 
4. Even if you get eliminated early, you can stay on this screen to follow the timer, chat with the group, and watch the event unfold!

---

## 3. The New Guest Experience
*A person without an account who was invited as someone's +1. You get a frictionless, web-only experience to participate without needing to sign up.*

### Claiming Your Spot
1. You will receive a link or QR code from your friend (the Registered Member).
2. Opening this link takes you to a friendly **Guest Landing Page**. You don't need an account!
3. Tap **Who invited you?** and select your friend's name (e.g., "Alex's Guest 1").
4. Enter your real name to claim the slot. This reservation is saved to your browser so you don't lose it.

### The Day of the Event
1. When you arrive at the venue, open that same link on your phone.
2. Tap **Check In**. The Admin will confirm you, and your screen will update with your **Table and Seat assignment**.
3. Tap **Open Live Tournament** to follow the game. 

### Guest Limitations
To keep the permanent group private, your Guest view is purposefully restricted:
* You **can** see the timer, blinds, average stack, and your own seating.
* You **cannot** see the group chat, group polls, or history.
* After the tournament ends, you will see the final placements, but no financial payout amounts.
* *Optional*: At the end of the night, you will see a prompt to create an account if you want to join the group permanently!
